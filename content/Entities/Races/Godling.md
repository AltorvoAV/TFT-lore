**TLDR**: Failed creation resembling the gods, created by the gods.

- Again another failed attempt of gods creating powerful life, or at least that is what we are led to believe - [[Atropal]] is our only reference point for a godling.
- According to surviving accounts, it was created by the gods to resemble themselves, possessing aspects of the Elders whilst using the mortal formula. The creation was ultimately rejected and murdered, its nature described in divine accounts as 'horrific, disgusting, and evil'.
	- Yet little is known beyond these accounts. Whether Atropal was born a monstrous abomination, became so through rejection, or was condemned for reasons its creators never revealed remains unknown. Some scholars question whether the surviving accounts describe the truth, or merely the judgement of those who created it.
- Upon [[Atropal]]'s death, [[Myrkul]] (newly god of death at the time) rejected the abomination, wanting no part of it to enter death - and as such, [[Atropal]] became the first undead, and the first being rejected from death itself.
- We now know [[Atropal]] as a vengeful evil foetus-like being, but again we only know what we’ve heard from clerics and the communions with gods telling their own opinion or perhaps further propaganda - one of those mysteries we may never know. Perhaps Atropal was the beginning of a creation but died or was killed during creation.
- [[Atropal]] is commonly classified as both a [[Quasi-Deity]] and a [[Vestige]]: a residual divine presence associated with a being believed to have died, yet which persists beyond the boundaries of ordinary existence. Yes, the classifications are imperfect. Atropal possesses a body, is remembered by the gods, and yet remains present in Nyropa, despite being neither truly alive nor dead.
	- This is purely our mortal attempt to explain a metaphysical anomaly.
