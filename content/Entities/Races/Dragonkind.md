### Basic Info:
[[Dragonkind]] came into beings during the birth of the [[Material Plane]]. Its said their design was a mix between [[Asgorath]] and the [[Elder Dragon]]. This would explain why the True Dragons appeared first, and their might much greater than others.

### History:
The first dragons to walk the mortal planes were the True Dragons, birthed during the creation of the [[Material Plane]] thanks to [[Selûne]] and [[Shar]].

During the next thousand or so years came the evolution and creation of the Lesser, and normal dragons, which some argue took on some of the traits of the original True Dragons.

Once interbreeding happened between [[Dragonkind]] and other mortal races, this led to the birth of new dragon-type creatures, namely Dragonborn, Half-Dragons, and Dracolisks.

It wasn't until [[Tiamat]] created her [[Dragonspawn]] that [[Koboldkind|Kobolds]] came into existence.
