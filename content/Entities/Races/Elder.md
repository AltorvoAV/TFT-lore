**TLDR**: Gods created following the [[Primordial|Primordials]] - ancient, unaging. Only two left which are known of. The precursors to mortal kind.

- The [[Elder|Elders]] came after the [[Primordial|Primordials]]. The first gods made outside of the beginning of time.
- Some believe the [[Elder|Elders]] are a race of what came before Humans, Elves…etc and all known mortal beings. It is generally believed the Elders were born in the [[Outer Planes]], and were an ancient unaging race - a prototype for all mortals. One theory holds that their endless lives were considered a 'flaw'; that without an end, existence lacked meaning. Others argue that this is merely a mortal reasoning imposed upon beings we can scarcely hope to understand.
- There are only two beings currently known of that are Elders:
    - [[Oghma]], god of Knowledge
    - [[Jergal]], former god of Death, now wandering the planes
- It is believed there were thousands if not millions of Elders, very few became gods, but almost all are believed to have perished. Speculation over the thousands of years has grown as to how they died, but almost all researchers have come to the conclusion we shalt never truly know. In fact, there was a surviving tome that spoke of three Elder gods: Oghma, Jergal, and a third whose name has been lost. We now recognise only two. Whether the discrepancy reflects a mistake in the old texts or something lost from history remains unknown~~, but I reckon Jared fucked up~~.
- It is also worth stating that there was a unique variation of the [[Elder]] race, which came after the creation of the original Elders. This of course being the [[Elder Dragon]] - a hybrid which took aspects of [[Asgorath]] and mixed them with the Elders. Though it is agreed within the research community that there was only one being who ever had this unique hybrid - so perhaps it was a failure or a success, again we will probably never know.
- Some believe the [[Old Ones]] are actually forgotten [[Elder]] beings who relinquished their divine domains - though this theory contradicts the common belief that Old Ones are a 'god-race' - either way, the 'Old Ones' are far too ancient to have any records or opinions outside of speculation.
	- Similarly, Elders have often been called '[[Elder|Immortals]]' in older surviving texts.
