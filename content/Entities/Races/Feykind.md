### Basic Info:
**[[Feykind]]** (or [[Feykind|Fae]], [[Feykind|Fey]], [[Feykind|Faekind]]) are magical, emotion-driven spirits and creatures native to the [[Feywild]]. Speaking Sylvan, they are deeply bound to nature and raw magic. 

To outsiders, the Fae and their realm appear whimsical and harmless—a world of pure prey. In truth, nothing in the [[Feywild]] is as it seems. Everything is predatory, and mortals only discover the danger once they break the Fae's unspoken rules.

* **Patron Deities:** [[Selûne]] (creator, prior to her death), [[Yondalla]], and [[Ehlonna]].

---

### Origins:
[[Feykind]] mirrors the chaotic, vibrant nature of its creator, [[Selûne]]. When she shaped the [[Feywild]] as an extravagant reflection of the [[Material Plane]], she poured her deepest, most sensitive emotions into it, leaving herself more subdued in the process. 

Naïve in the art of creation, Selûne made EVERYTHING sentient, potent, and filled with Wild Magic. First emerged two of her daughters, [[Yondalla]] and [[Ehlonna]], gods in their own right, followed by the first Archfey, Dryads, and Nymphs. For the first few thousand years, many Fae did not realise they were alive, until they discovered they could step out of the trees, glades, and waters they inhabited to take physical form, speech, and movement.

* **Rebirth & Nature:** [[Feykind|Fae]] do not experience life linearly. When a Fae grows bored of mortal-like existence, they can dissolve back into nature; a tree, a glade, or a body of water. Then reform into a preferred shape whenever they wish to walk again.

### Hierarchy & The Courts:
[[Feykind|Fae]] society is built on prestige, favours, oaths, and etiquette rather than rigid mortal law.
* **High Fae:** The 'elite' capable of ruling consisting of
	* **Archfey**: (god-like beings capable of commanding domains and granting Warlock pacts)
	* **True Fae**: Sapient, nature-born Fae with full intellect, courtly ambition, and mastery over Glamour (such as Dryads, Nymphs, Satyrs, and Hags).
	* **[[Elvenkind|Elves]]**: [[Corellon Larethian|Corellon]]'s creations who earned their place among the courts over millennia thanks to [[Raven Queen|The First Elven Queen]].
* **Lesser Fae:** Beast-like creatures, hybrids, and elemental nature spirits (such as sprites, pixies, and squinks).

#### The Twin Courts:
* **The Seelie Court (*Summer Court*):** Governs big-picture [[Feywild]] decisions. Currently ruled by **[[Yondalla]]**, with her sister **[[Ehlonna]]** holding a seat (though she rarely appears).
	* Notable Members:
		* [[Yondalla]], [[Lord Squark Arrowvis, Earl of Peckersberg|Lord Squark]], [[Drachen Lokeriyan]] (has since absconded from his duties), the 8th Elven Queen, and [[Duchess of Tern]].
* **The Unseelie Court (*Gloaming / Winter Court*):** Handles external relations with other pantheons and their influence on the Feywild. Originally created by [[Selûne]] to honour her sister **[[Shar]]** (who hated it), it is now ruled by **[[Titania, Queen of the Fairies]]** (who moved from the Seelie Court after millennia, though she still steps into help on some Seelie matters) and her consort **[[Oberon, King of the Fairies]]** (an ancient awakened forest spirit who watches over wild beasts).
	* Notable Members:
		* [[Titania, Queen of the Fairies|Titania]], [[Oberon, King of the Fairies|Oberon]], The Beauchêne Family, [[Anherra]].

### Social Rules & Etiquette:
* **Incapable of Lying:** Most [[Feykind|Fae]] physically cannot speak an outright falsehood. Instead, they are masters of half-truths, riddles, and wordplay; a skill that becomes seamless the older a Fae grows.
* **Names & Gifts:** Giving a [[Feykind|Fey]] your true name grants them binding power over you (an ancient magic tied to the gods beholden to [[Oblivati Mori]]). Similarly, never accept a free gift or food from a Fae, as it creates an automatic, binding magical debt.
* **Glamour:** [[Feykind|Fae]] rarely wear their true faces, masking themselves and their surroundings in illusion. Mortals pierce this veil by viewing a Fae's reflection in cold water or iron, coating their eyes in special ointments, or looking through enchanted glass.
* **The Wild Hunt:** Led by the Archfey **[[The Horned King]]**, this unstoppable spectral cavalry rides across the realm. Anything caught in its path is either slain or forced to join the eternal ride.

---

### Fae vs. Elves:
Though both were born at the dawn of the [[Feywild]], [[Feykind]] and [[Elvenkind]] have fought countless wars over the millennia. Many [[Feykind|Fey]] viewed themselves as the true rulers of the plane and dismissed [[Elvenkind|Elves]] as stiff, overly structured upstarts. Over time, as more Elves evolved into seasonal [[Elvenkind|Fey Eladrin]], that prejudice faded; largely thanks to **[[Raven Queen|The First Elven Queen]]**, who worked tirelessly to bridge the divide and broker lasting peace between the two cultures.

### Folklore & Unconfirmed Myths:
Mortals tell many tales about how to survive the [[Feykind|Fae]]; truth and exaggeration blur together:
* **Cold Iron:** Legend claims cold iron burns Fae flesh on contact; many mortals line their homes with iron to ward them off.
* **Changelings:** Rumours persist that Fae steal mortal babies and leave sickly Fae doubles or changelings in their place. A Fae once argued this was a myth in a language so convoluted no one could tell if they were telling the truth.
* **The Tithe to Hell (Teind):** Some whisper that to maintain their agelessness, Fae must pay a tribute every seven years; kidnapping living mortal souls to offer to fiendish beings.
* **Compulsions:** Folklore claims Fae cannot cross running water, must stop to count every grain of spilled salt or seeds thrown in their path, and are compelled to untie complex knots before chasing prey.

---

### Feywild Magic & Emotions:
*(See also: [[Feywild]])*
* **Weaponised Emotion:** Emotions in the [[Feywild]] are amplified to their absolute extreme. They shape reality—an Archfey's shifting mood instantly alters the weather, landscape, and gravity of their personal pocket realm ('**Domains of Delight**').
* **Memories as Currency:** Because emotions carry physical and magical weight, [[Feykind|Fey]] can feed on them. Consequently, memories and abstract experiences are commonly used as payment in Fae bargains.

### Notable Fauna & Subspecies:
* **Feathered Squinks** *(Notes from [[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]])*: Small, amphibious fae slightly larger than pixies, resembling a cross between a grung and an axolotl. A feathered subspecies undergoes a coming-of-age ritual where they leap from floating cliffs for their first flight—and not one has ever failed, as both land and air accept them. Eating a squink is both fatal (they are violently poisonous) and deeply sacrilegious; a group who once ate them slowly dissolved into mush over 300 years.

### Notable Figures:
* **[[Yondalla]]:** Daughter of Selûne, Goddess of Life, and current ruler of the Seelie Court.
* **[[Ehlonna]]:** Sister to Yondalla; holds a rarely occupied seat on the Seelie Court.
* **[[Titania, Queen of the Fairies]]:** Former Seelie ruler who now governs the Unseelie Court alongside her husband, Oberon.
* **[[Oberon, King of the Fairies]]:** Ancient awakened spirit of a great forest; rules the Unseelie Court with Titania and watches over wild beasts.
* **[[The Horned King]]:** Fearsome Archfey who commands the Wild Hunt.
* **[[Lord Squark Arrowvis, Earl of Peckersberg]]:** An avian noble of the Seelie Court involved in the politics of the *Lords of the Wing*, the *Court of Wonder*, and the grand wager of the annual Bloom organised by **[[Delloso Dellarue]]**.
* **[[Duchess of Tern]]**: Former [[Elvenkind|Fey Eladrin]], now prawn (turned during mid-debate on the Seelie Court).
