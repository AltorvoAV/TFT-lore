### Basic Info:
Created by [[Tiamat]], the [[Dragonspawn]] are a type of creature moulded in the image of true Dragons; keeping parts of their traits, and inheriting new ones. [[Dragonspawn]] is not the same as [[Dragonkind]], as Dragonkind refers to all those Dragon-like creatures not just the ones created by Tiamat.

### Species created:
- [[Koboldkind]], and [[Kurtulmak]]
- Urds
- Spawn from each chromatic colour:
	- Redspawn
	- Blackspawn
	- Bluespawn
	- Greenspawn
	- Whitespawn

### History:
It is believed [[Tiamat]] created these species during an ancient great war of some kind, though the specifics have misinterpreted, misremembered, and misplaced over the past thousand or so years. Therefore, we can only speculate over the intention and eventual creation of such species.
