### Basic Info:
After the creation of the [[Feywild]] by [[Selûne]]/[[Shar]], [[Corellon Larethian]] birthed life which wandered out from its beautiful glades. Though some argue [[Elvenkind]] was made out of the first divine blood shed from [[Corellon Larethian]] during his early wars against [[Gruumsh]].

Though mortal, it is commonly known that whilst living in the plane of their original creation ([[Feywild]]), they cannot die of old age.

### Species:
- The [[Feywild]] Origins:
	- **High Elves** (*Common Eladrin*)
	-  **Fey Eladrin** (*Noble Eladrin*): The most powerful subspecies of the Eladrin, fused with the powers within the [[Feywild]] and adopting powers related to its seasons
		- Spring Eladrin
		- Summer Eladrin
		- Autumn Eladrin
		- Winter Eladrin
- The [[Material Plane]] Subspecies:
	- **Wood Elves** (*Copper Elves*): Descendants of High Elves/Eladrin, born and evolved in the [[Material Plane]]
	- Material Subspecies of High Elves:
		- **Sun Elves** (*Gold Elves*)
		- **Moon Elves** (*Silver Elves*)
	- **Sea Elves** (*Aquatic Elves*)
	- **Drow** (_Dark elves_): Fallen Fey Eladrin of a severed fifth season (_Eclipse & Blight_), cursed and banished into the [[Underdark]] after [[Lolth]]'s rebellion
- Planar & Extraplanar Elves
	- **Astral Elves** (*Star Elves*): though originally from the [[Feywild]], they spent thousands of years living in the [[Astral Plane-Sea]], and thus changed by its timeless powers into Astral Elves
	- **Shadar-Kai** (*Shadow Elves*): Though originally from the [[Feywild]], they were cast into [[Shadowfell]] with their newly formed goddess, the [[Raven Queen]] - thus becoming Shadar-Kai

### History:
The first [[Elvenkind|Elves]] were far more powerful than modern elves. They shared innate abilities and the gift of powerful spell casting, similar to [[Corellon Larethian]] himself. Some of these individuals are still somewhat known of to this day:
- [[Raven Queen|The First Elven Queen]]
- [[Lolth]]

The closest living relatives to these original shape-shifting elves are Fey Eladrin. Much older information has been lost over the tens of thousands of years since their creation.

Before the Crown Wars (a devastating ancient civil war), a fifth subspecies of Fey Eladrin embodied a dark season: _Eclipse & Blight_. When [[Lolth]] rebelled against the elven rulers, her followers joined her. Betrayed, [[Corellon Larethian]] cast Lolth into [[The Abyss]] and ripped that dark season from the [[Feywild]] forever. To keep her followers separated from their fallen goddess, Corellon stripped their immortality and banished them beneath the [[Material Plane]], where isolation reshaped them into the **Drow** of the [[Underdark]].

After some time, thousands of years after their creation, [[Elvenkind]] wandered out and began to explore the other realms. At some point, many made their way to the [[Material Plane]] where many Elves still live to this day, which led to many differing breeds of Half-Elf and eventually the birth of new subspecies: Wood Elves, Sun Elves, Moon Elves, and Sea Elves.
