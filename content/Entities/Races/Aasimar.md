**TLDR**: Part god, mortal, created to spread the faith and power.

- [[Aasimar]] were originally a voice of the gods, a fraction of the original god’s power contained within a mortal, created to spread the god’s teachings and convert more to their faith.
- A creation birthed from part mortal, part god.
- An Aasimar inherits more than a fraction of divine power, however. They inherit the potential and right to godhood held by their divine parent, including the right to their parent's domain. Should that god fall, their Aasimar offspring may succeed them, preserving the divine domain rather than leaving it open to another claimant.
	- Both fortunately and unfortunately (from a science perspective), we have never seen such a ritual be complete.
	- Few Aasimar are known to have turned against their divine nature. [[Lucifer Morningstar|The Harbinger]] is one such example.
