**TLDR**: The first beings created at the beginning of time to be gods. Currently known as: [[Selûne]], [[Shar]], and [[Asgorath]].

- Created at the beginning of time. It is often believed there was a being who created all that we know, and all those that have been. And that this being created all of the gods. Their first creations were time and space. The first life they brought forth from oblivion was a pair of sisters. One who created light and her twin, who created shadows. Both born of nothing but primordial energy and gifted Domains and the power that we now know as being a Greater Deity.
- They were not the only beings created in this moment, another a more powerful being who we only know by name: [[Asgorath]] - a being so strong they could shape new universes and life within it.
- No other Primordial beings have ever been recorded or witnessed. Some say that if there was truly a being who created everything, surely it would itself be a primordial, but others say it would be a contradiction as the creation itself bore life to primordial energy - so if there were a being before time, they would be something else entirely.
