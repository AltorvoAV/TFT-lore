## Powerful Beings
Individuals who were not quite given the title of Godhood, yet possess power on-par with [[Lesser Deity|Lesser-Deities]], [[Quasi-Deity|Quasi-Deities]], [[Archfey]], or [[Archfiends]]. Often these are [[Aasimar|Aasimars]], Liches, or other significant beings. It takes great power to contest that of a God.
