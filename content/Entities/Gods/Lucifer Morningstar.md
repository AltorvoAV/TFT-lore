## Description:
As described by [[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]]'s [[Patron]]:

A once kind, devoted, and generous soul, with a heart full of love. He was known for his countless acts of bravery, selflessness, and altruism. He was often thought of as an [[Lucifer Morningstar|Angel]] sent down by the gods themselves to help save [[Nyropa]] and all its [[Realms|realms]].

Though at some point within his long lifetime, he cracked, and nothing could save those around him.

## Relationships
Little is currently known about [[Lucifer Morningstar|Morningstar]] and his relationships to other beings, however deep inside [[Lucifer Morningstar|Lucifer]]'s keep inside [[The Lost Lair of Souls - The Feted Fortress|The Feted Fortress]] was [[Barrus St Claire]].

[[Barrus St Claire|Barrus]] spoke highly and subserviently of [[Lucifer Morningstar|Lucifer]]; mentioning that "[[Lucifer Morningstar]] pities you [[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]]". And calling [[Lucifer Morningstar|Lucifer]] his '*Master*'.

Further, it was confirmed by both [[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]]'s [[Patron]] & the [[Sword of Sorrow]], that [[Lucifer Morningstar|Morningstar]] was the previous owner of said [[Artefacts|Artefact]]. [[Sword of Sorrow|Sorrow]] could only refer to [[Lucifer Morningstar|Lucifer]] by the term '[[Lucifer Morningstar|Morningstar]]' due to him being shrouded by [[Oblivati Mori]], which is now broken for the [[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]]& [[Ayanna Everstride|Ayanna]], as they were told the name directly by [[Barrus St Claire|Barrus]]. It is known that [[Andaraleanbh Beauchêne]] defeated the previous wielder of [[Sword of Sorrow|Sorrow]], therefore it can be assumed that [[Andaraleanbh Beauchêne|Andaraleanbh]] defeated [[Lucifer Morningstar|The Harbinger]] himself.
