## Description:
[[Tiamat]] is the Goddess of Greed and Queen of Evil Dragons. Her domains extend beyond avarice into temptation, tyranny, domination, and ownership.

Tiamat believes the world is naturally hierarchical, that power establishes authority, and that everything of sufficient value will eventually belong to whoever possesses the strength to claim it.

She is the eternal rival of [[Bahamut]], her mirror among the metallic dragons. Where Bahamut represents a form of ordered goodness, Tiamat represents ordered domination. Where the structure ultimately serves her.

> "*Everything, even existence is mine, by right.*"
> 
> — [[Tiamat]]

---
### Appearance
[[Tiamat]] appears as a five-headed chromatic dragon, each head displaying the primary colours of the most common species of chromatic dragons: Blue, Black, Red, White, and Green.

She is a Gargantuan Fiendish Creature, with a giant tail ending in a venomous stinger. A feature sometimes cited by [Nyropan](Nyropa) draconic scholars as an origin for the wyvern's characteristic tail.

#### Avatar(s)
[[Tiamat]] has little interest in subtlety when presenting herself. To Tiamat, appearing as anything less than the [[Tiamat|Queen of Evil Dragons]] is beneath her.

Her true form may be divine, incomprehensible, or beyond mortal perception, but when she chooses to be seen, she wants mortals to understand exactly who they are looking at.

## Personality
Pride, arrogance, vanity, spite, pettiness, and greed define the [[Tiamat|Bane of Bahamut]]. But beneath that lies something deeper: *certainty.*

Tiamat does not question whether she will eventually rule. She considers it inevitable.

There are rulers and subjects, owners and possessions, creditors and debtors, masters and servants. A linear structure leading to [[Tiamat]]'s authority over all.

She is cold, demanding, and intolerant of weakness or incompetence. She remembers every insult and holds grudges for centuries.

Yet Tiamat is not incapable of affection. She can genuinely respect a worthy rival, reward a loyal servant, honour an ally, or even form friendships. However, this favour does not make someone her equal.

Tiamat is a capable diplomat and negotiator. She prefers a useful alliance to an unnecessary war, and a binding contract to a battlefield.

Her hoard is gathered through her followers, allowing her to remain close to what she values most. Wealth, territory, influence, dragons, artefacts, servants, and kingdoms are all simply different forms of possession. All aiming to satisfy her insatiable hunger.

She respects hierarchy, to dominate it; authority to possess it; law to define ownership, obligation and consequence.

## Worshipers
> "*Many mouths speak your hunger into this world, mistress. Let me be the loudest.*"
> 
> — A basic prayer to [[Tiamat]]

Members of the [[Church of Tiamat]], or [[Tiamat|Tiamatan]]s, are devout religious zealots drawn to power, wealth, revenge, status, and domination. They are often people who suffer powerlessness, and resentment, seeing the world as injust and unfair. The [[Tiamat|Queen of Evil Dragons]] speaks of the strong *taking* what they want and promises those who join her, will never feel powerless again.

The Church mirrors [[Tiamat]]'s own ideals, maintaining a strict hierarchy of rank and authority. It uses the colours of the most common chromatic dragons. From lowest to highest:
- White Adepts,
- Black Adepts,
- Green Adepts,
- Blue Adepts,
- Red Adepts,
- High Priest (of a temple or sect),
- [[Tiamat]]'s Chosen (Leader of the church/cult & voice of [[Tiamat]])

Warlocks who receive power from Tiamat often enter contracts requiring wealth, service, or magical offerings in return.

The faithful believe when [[Tiamat]] returns, her chromatic children will become her dukes and her clergy will become her vassals.
#### Dragons & [[Koboldkind|Kobolds]]
Many evil dragons have worshiped [[Tiamat]] since dragons first appeared in [[Nyropa]] and its [[Realms|Planes]].

Many [[Koboldkind|Kobolds]] have historically regarded her as their creator, due to many accounts stating [[Tiamat]] created [[Kurtulmak]]. Among some Kobold communities, the birth of a Dragonwrought Kobold is considered an omen of [[Tiamat]]'s favour and servitude towards the [[Tiamat|Archdevil]] herself.
#### Tenets
- **Power belongs to the powerful.** - Weakness deserves exploitation.
- **Wealth should belong to those strong enough to claim it.** - Theft is not immoral when the victim cannot prevent it.
- **Dragons are superior to lesser beings.** - The strong were born to rule the weak.
- **Tiamat deserves obedience.** - Her commands are not suggestions.
- **Other gods are rivals.** - Their followers, temples and influence compete with [[Tiamat]]'s rightful dominion.
#### Pursuits
- Wealth · Influence · Magic · Military Power · Prestige · Treasure · Servants

Every acquisition is an act of devotion. Every throne claimed, debt collected, treasure stolen and rival defeated adds to Tiamat's hoard. [[Tiamat|Tiamatan]]s have little reason to conceal their allegiance. They believe their Goddess will eventually rule openly, and see no shame in serving the power they believe will inherit the world.
#### Goal
The [[Church of Tiamat]]'s primary goal is to free [[Tiamat]] from the [[Nine Hells]] and back into the [[Material Plane]]. Rumours spread about the methods of how. Some claim uniting the 5 draconic masks, turning them into the [[Mask of the Dragon Queen]], can release her from prison in the [[Nine Hells]]. Others believe any other god can summon her, but it would take immense power.

## Abilities
Those who saw the [[Tiamat|Archdevil]] fight, claimed her heads were able to cooperate in battle, and that those heads had the ability to use the breath weapons common to their coloured species. Her stinger injected a powerful venom on her foes, killing them in agonizing pain.

It is believed she is immune to the damages typically associated with the colours she inhabits. To oppose her with the chromatic elements is to feed her power.

[[Tiamat]]'s commands carry the crushing weight of absolute authority. It is said that her spoken word alone possesses the divine kinetic force to banish planar-interlopers, forcefully casting non-native beings back to their birth plane, simply because she refuses to share her territory with uninvited guests.
### Weaknesses
Originating from the [[Material Plane]], Tiamat is [[Ontology|ontologically]] distinct from the gods that hailed from the [[Outer Planes]] or [[Inner Planes]] (excluding the [[Material Plane|Material]]), rendering her possible to kill, as she is not protected by [[Oblivati Mori]].

## Relationships
[[Tiamat|The Many-Mawed]]'s eternal rival was her brother, [[Bahamut]]. Their strive has existed since the birth of dragons, a resentment of their differing beliefs and aims, which trickled down into their worshippers. She, his antithesis; the [[Tiamat|Bane of Bahamut]]. 

Though she saw [[Jergal]] as an enemy, for she coveted his domain, [[Tiamat]] grew an alliance with the former god of Death, Tyranny, & Murder, and served him until his gave up his domains. [[Tiamat]] then forged a contractual alliance with his successor, [[Bane]], to gain a portion of his Tyranny domain.

After creation of her [[Dragonspawn]], the god of the kobolds, [[Kurtulmak]], was her servant for a significant time.

[[Tiamat]] spent many years as an Archfiend, serving [[Asmodeus]], living in the [[Nine Hells]]. She grew many alliances, was used, and betrayed by others, but followed [[Nine Hells|Hell]]'s politics eventually becoming courteous to [[Asmodeus]] and other Archfiends, to her advantage.

She maintains a consort from each chromatic dragon races, urging her evil draconic followers to populate the world and fill it with her spawn.

In each of her bouts serving another being, she dutifully accepted, without accepting their superiority.

## The Dragonfall Wars

Tiamat and Bahamut became the central figures in an ancient conflict between chromatic and metallic dragonkind.

Tiamat created horrific draconic creatures—the Spawn of Tiamat—in an attempt to destroy Bahamut's followers.

The conflict became one of the defining religious wars of ancient dragonkind.


[[History of the Forgotten War|The Great Forgotten War]]
Tiamat and Bahamut eventually fought in the Battle of the Gods.

Both were killed in their Untheric aspects.

Tiamat lost her divine status and became an archfiend.

This period of relative mortality profoundly affected her.

It made her more cautious and paranoid.

But it didn't make her less ambitious.

If anything, it taught her that **even Tiamat could lose.**

And that lesson stayed with her.
