### **Core Themes:**
- Balance and cosmic order
- Creation and existence
- Divine Authority

### **What [[AO]] Desires:**
- The preservation of balance throughout existence.
- To expose corruption and weakness within the systems of creation, including the gods themselves.
- To ensure that existence fulfils its intended purpose, even if doing so requires the destruction of what already exists.
- To identify and correct that which threatens the balance of creation.
