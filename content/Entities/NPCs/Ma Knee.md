**Quick Info:**
- Race: #Halfling 
- Age: 12
- Occupation: Child
- Met: On the road to [[The Lost Lair of Souls - The Feted Fortress|The Feted Fortress]]
- Status: #Dead 

Traded to a group of bandits by their mother [[Quiggle]], as the bandits asked for her 'Money' which she thought sounded like [[Ma Knee]] (the name given to her child due to them being the height of her knee).

[[Ma Knee]] was briefly rescued from the bandits by the [[Player Characters|Moonlit Syndicate]], only to then be murdered by [[Drachen Lokeriyan]], who then partially ate the child's innards.
