A perfect specimen; 5-time eating champion, who '*always finishes first*'.

His opening gambit shook fear into [[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]] upon their first meeting: '*Come to challenge me in a feast, have you? Are your loins prepared? For I am Maximilian Slickenbottom*'

With a high-born accent and a high-calorie figure, [[Maximilian Slickenbottom]] was no easy contest, using the no-hands, full-face technique, [[Maximilian Slickenbottom]]'s victory was surely golden brown. However, [[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]] devoured the pie like he would a small child, and thus [[Maximilian Slickenbottom]] rose pie-covered, and titleless.

[[Maximilian Slickenbottom]] suddenly needed to prepare his loins, and as such waddled to the outhouses ([[Drachen Lokeriyan|Drachen]] following suit).

Drachen wrestled in the toilet for a solid 40 long exhausting minutes, emptying the hole in his belly, to fill one in the ground - and emerging visibly more gaunt and thin.

Though, he noticed there was no noise coming from [[Maximilian Slickenbottom]]'s outhouse, so he knocked, and eventually opened the door, only to find [[Maximilian Slickenbottom]] dead on the dunny.
