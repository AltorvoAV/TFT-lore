**Quick Info:**
- Race: #Shadar-Kai 
- Age: 26
- Occupation: Vanguard & Leader of the Vanguard Defence League ([[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthcan]] Defence)
- Met: N/A
- Status: #Dead (Killed by the inaction of a certain [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthcan]]: [[Devlin Shandian]])


Though not the chosen warrior of the [[Raven Queen]], the [[Lana Everstride|Axe of Porthca]] was a formidable warrior and fierce protector of the [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthcan]] people.

In her early years, [[Lana Everstride|Lana]] and her sister [[Ayanna Everstride|Ayanna]] would train in the art of combat across the largest mountains within the [[Shadowfell]] (geographically over [[The Anvils of Zaghar]]). There were inseparable for their whole lives... that was until the [[Raven Queen]] asked them to depart from their home and venture to the [[Material Plane|Material]].

Though [[Ayanna Everstride|Ayanna]] was chosen to become the inevitable '[[Ayanna Everstride|Blade of Nyropa]]', [[Lana Everstride|Lana]] was chosen to integrate with the [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthcan]] military months prior to the war that broke out between [[Brugrove - City of the Iron Guard|Brugrove]] and [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthca]]. There she became known as the [[Lana Everstride|Axe of Porthca]]. Apparently, she was only sent to [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthca]] because Porthca's original and 'most-qualified' protector had died some years prior.

**Fun Fact:**
[[Lana Everstride|Lana]]'s original name was [[Lana Everstride|Alanna]]. She changed it due to the constant confusion when news would arrive for '*A. Everstride*' and the sisters could never figure out who it was meant for: [[Ayanna Everstride|Ayanna]] or [[Lana Everstride|Alanna]].
