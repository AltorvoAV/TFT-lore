A talented mage from [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthca]] who ventured North East to explore [[Brugrove - City of the Iron Guard|Brugrove]], [[Valonde - The Pearled Coast|Valonde]], and other cities as part of [[The Sunrise Establishment (WWFtM)]]. During his time, he gained great knowledge, riches, and fame... far from where he came from.

Having studied at the [[College of Porthca]] in the art of adventuring, [[Archmage Serla Narren|Serla]] rose to the great heights of Archmage.

We now follow his journey across [[Nyropa]] into the [[Shazahath Mountains - The Ruined Caverns|Shazahath Mountains]] with [[The Sunrise Establishment (WWFtM)]].

## Background
[[Archmage Serla Narren|Serla]] grew up in House Narren, as son to wise and famous scholar - his mother was killed when travelling back from a relief mission to [[The Vale of Omens]], where she was killed by a ship of pirate goblins.

[[Archmage Serla Narren|Serla]] was guided towards magical learning by his father at the [[College of Porthca]], in hopes to fill the void left by his mother - but all that resulted in was [[Archmage Serla Narren|Serla]]'s discontent for certain beings festering more, with the only avenue he had for vengeance being through use of [[The Weave]].

As such, [[Archmage Serla Narren|Serla]] used his anger, his resentment, and his more eccentric side to dive deep into arcane lore and mysteries. He joined the military of [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthca]], and used his arcane knowledge as a Battle Mage.

His actions were so devastating, yet also revolutionary (at the time), that he was awarded the highest non-political title of 'Archmage' back in 45BR (at the age of 16, youngest Archmage of [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthca]] ever).

[[Archmage Serla Narren|Serla]] fought across the wars of the Southern side for decades, setting up camps using those he defeated as its workforce, until his magic waned following the aftermath of [[History of the Forgotten War|The Great Forgotten War]]. His curiousity was peaked. And due to him never finding those goblins that took his mother away from him; he set out to the Eastern side of Nyropa in search of what terrible happening caused his magic to falter.
