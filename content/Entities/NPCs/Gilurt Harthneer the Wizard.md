[[Gilurt Harthneer the Wizard]] became a Wizard, aged 12 after eating a friend on accident who was pretending to be a sandwich. Eating him, consuming his power and thus becoming a wizard. Though this may've been a distressing time, one can only hope [[Jeremiah]], his pet/familiar crow, helped him through this time.

[[Gilurt Harthneer the Wizard|Gilurt]] found himself in [[Ashlake]] looking for his student [[Maximilian Slickenbottom]]. Upon hearing of his death, [[Gilurt Harthneer the Wizard|Gilurt]] loudly exclaimed: 'Ah, I will find him, and eat him; consume his power.'

Although appearing out of nowhere, [[Gilurt Harthneer the Wizard|Gilurt]] showed great magical potential most notably by his rambunctious display of multiple 9th and one 8th level spell: 'Tsunami, tsunami, tsunami, Meteor Swarm, Clone (to which he rolled a 2, and scholars still to this day haven't a fuck to what that means)'.

[[Gilurt Harthneer the Wizard|Gilurt]] was no professor, but he was 14th of his name.
