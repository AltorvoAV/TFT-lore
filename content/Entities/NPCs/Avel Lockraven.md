## His story
[[Avel Lockraven|Avel]] young and hopeful for the world, walked with a pure heart, surrounded by greed and utter disregard for humanity: his brother, [[Ayers Lockraven|Ayers]].

Over the years, [[Avel Lockraven|Avel]] watched his brother belittle and manipulate, control and covet. Where [[Avel Lockraven|Avel]] would give second chances to dock workers and business partners, [[Ayers Lockraven|Ayers]] would remind [[Avel Lockraven|Avel]] that these 'low-lifes' mean nothing, and to treat them as such. Though his brother was never outwardly violent, his actions and disguised speech made it clear he wanted nothing but harm to come to those below 'his station'.

Having been the best man at his brother's wedding, in marriage to [[Tallia Lockraven|Tallia]], [[Avel Lockraven|Avel]] saw they were thick and thieves. She understood his brother's twisted mindset, that: image is the only thing that matters. Before [[Avel Lockraven|Avel]] took his place alongside his brother, he realised he would find no happiness nor feeling of contentment around the Lockraven name. He wanted a life of peace and meaning.

So when the manor was nothing but candle-light and snores, [[Avel Lockraven|Avel]] used his knowledge of the trade routes to smuggle himself out, aiming to disappear and find a simple life.

After [[Avel Lockraven|Avel]]'s money was running low, he looked anywhere for jobs to do something meaningful. And that's when he found a small modest carpenter family of the last name 'Quade'. They took him in, asked nothing about his past and showed him kindness and love. In return he offered them nothing but his ear, his hands, hid mind, and his heart. He became [[Avel Lockraven|Avel Quade]]. They taught him humility, work, and love without conditions.

Over the next 10 or so years, [[Avel Lockraven|Avel Quade]] created his own business '[[Quade & Co|Quade Carpentry]]', where he created beautiful hand-crafted, artisan clocks, music boxes, violins...etc. He became known for his skill and generosity, rather than his name. There was the heir of mystery behind his creations, as he always feared his brother discovering him, so he set up dead-drops, night deliveries. All in aims to keep his life, his.

[[Avel Lockraven|Avel]]'s knowledge of the trade networks only bolstered his business. Out of distaste for money, he would fix children's wooden toys for free, donate to veteran charities, for widows, and to [[Garl Glittergold]]'s temples in hopes for the fortune of his own family one day.

A wish that eventually came true. By age 31, he married a beautiful and kind tiefling; [[Meredith Vassargo]]. She was a skilled artisan jeweller in her own right. And so, [[Quade & Co|Quade Carpentry]] became [[Quade & Co]].

A few years later, a child followed; [[Rodwin Vassargo]].

And to this day, only his wife, daughter, and single trade-contact know of his true birthname.
