### Basic Info:
The **[[Feywild]]** is a vibrant, chaotic echo of the [[Material Plane]], brimming with untamed nature, rampant Wild Magic, and heightened emotion. Born from a clash between [[Selûne]] and [[Shar]] and shaped by Selûne's hand, it serves as the ancestral homeland of both [[Feykind]] and [[Elvenkind]].

* **Plane Type:** Echo Plane (mirrors the geography of the [[Material Plane]] in an extravagant, exaggerated form).
* **Creators:** [[Selûne]] and [[Shar]].
* **God(s) with Dominion over the Realm**: [[Yondalla]]
* **Rulers:** Governed jointly by the **Seelie Court** (led by [[Yondalla]]) and the **Unseelie Court** (led by [[Titania, Queen of the Fairies|Titania]] & [[Oberon, King of the Fairies|Oberon]]).
* **Alignment:** *Such a mortal notion, but if you had to define it*: Chaotic Neutral (driven by impulse, emotion, and etiquette rather than mortal good or evil).
* **Native Languages:** Sylvan, Elvish.

---

### Description & Environment:
To outsiders, the [[Feywild]] appears breathtakingly lush and harmless. Filled with towering ancient forests, floating cliffs, glowing glades, and vibrant skies. Yet, nothing here is as it seems. Everything is sentient, predatory, and bound by unspoken rules (See [[Feykind]] for more details).

* **Exaggerated Geography:** Mountains rise higher, forests grow denser, and colours burn brighter than their counterparts on the [[Material Plane]].
* **Domains of Delight:** The plane is fractured into personal pocket realms controlled by powerful Archfey. An Archfey's shifting mood instantly alters the weather, landscape, and gravity of their domain.
* **Wild Magic:** Raw, chaotic magic saturates the air and land, poured into the realm by [[Selûne]] and one of her daughters at the dawn of its creation. There have been accounts where the outcomes of spells were... unpredictable.
* **The Feydark:** Just as the [[Underdark]] stretches beneath the [[Material Plane]], the [[Feywild]] possesses its own subterranean echo known as the [[Feywild|Feydark]]. Far below the vibrant surface glades lies a sprawling network of glowing fungal caverns, crystal chasms, and lightless rivers. Home to exiled Fomorians, burrowing Korreds, and darker subterranean Fey.

---

### Planar Laws & Magic:
* **Agelessness:** While residing inside the [[Feywild]], members of [[Elvenkind]] and [[Feykind]] cannot die of old age. Though [[Feykind|Fey]] very rarely spend their whole lives in the same body.
* **Weaponised Emotion:** Emotions are amplified to their absolute extreme and carry physical weight. [[Feykind|Fae]] can feed on strong feelings, thus making memories and abstract experiences a form of currency.
* **Illusion & Glamour:** Landscapes and creatures rarely show their true forms, masking dangers behind layers of magical Glamour.
* **Planar Travel & Time:** Portals (Fey Crossings) open only under poetic conditions, such as stepping through a mushroom ring under a full moon, whilst singing a forgotten tune. Without a proper guide, time can warp unpredictably upon leaving the realm's borders. Days spent in the [[Feywild]] can equal minutes or centuries on the [[Material Plane]].

---

### Governance & Rulers:
Though the [[Feywild]] is typically seen as having no single monarch, [[Yondalla]] does occupy dominion over this realm. However, for the general running, power is split between two rival courts and independent Archfey:
* **The Seelie Court (*Summer Court*)** & **The Unseelie Court (*Gloaming / Winter Court*)** (See more in [[Feykind]])
	* The Seelie Court is sometimes held within [[Ysgard]] instead, to pay homage to [[Selûne]]. 
* **Independent Powers:** Archfey such as **[[The Horned King]]** (leader of the Wild Hunt) command their own territories and forces outside strict court control.

### Notable Inhabitants:
* **[[Feykind]]:** The native spirits and creatures born directly from [[Selûne]]'s magic, spanning:
	* **High Fae:** Archfey and True Fae (Fairies, Dryads, Nymphs, Satyrs, Hags, and Korreds).
		* [[Elvenkind|Elves]] are also considered High Fae, thanks to [[Raven Queen|The First Elven Queen]].
	* **Lesser Fae:** Beast-like creatures, hybrids, Redcaps, Fomorians, Treants, Sprites, Pixies, and Squinks.
* **[[Elvenkind]]:** 
	* Born here through [[Corellon Larethian]], though many later migrated to other planes. Those who remained evolved into seasonal [[Elvenkind|Fey Eladrin]] and [[Elvenkind|High Elves]].
* **Fey-Touched & Beastfolk Races:** 
	* Sapient mortal lineages native to the plane, including Aarakocra, Centaurs, Firbolgs, Harengon, Kenku, Owlin, and Tabaxi.
* **Sylvan Beasts & Monsters:** 
	* Blink dogs, displacer beasts, giant owls, giant eagles, unicorns, and awakened beasts roam the wilds.
* **Deities:** 
	* **[[Yondalla]]:** Spends most of her time here, having emerged from the plane's creation.
	* **[[Ehlonna]]:** Used to spend all her time in the [[Beastlands]]. However, since its destruction, she now spends most of her time within the [[Feywild]].
	* **[[Selûne]]:** Though its creator, she spent most of her time in [[Ysgard]], treating that as her main dominion and the [[Feywild]] as her second; perhaps to allow [[Yondalla]] to one day take her place.

### Brief History:
* **Creation:**
	* During an ancient battle, [[Shar]] hurled a devastating bolt of primordial magic at [[Selûne]]. The blast grazed Selûne, sending a volatile spiral of their combined magic careening between realms, eventually exploding, thus creating the [[Material Plane]]. 
	* From that cosmic impact, two raw echoes of the Material budded into existence. Neither goddess intended to create the other's realm. [[Selûne]] reached into the brighter echo and shaped it into the [[Feywild]], pouring her deepest emotions and Wild Magic into the land, while [[Shar]] claimed and twisted the darker echo into the [[Shadowfell]].
* **The Dawn Wars:**
	* Early [[Feykind]] and [[Elvenkind]] clashed over dominance of the realm for millennia until **[[Raven Queen|The First Elven Queen]]** brokered lasting peace.
* **The Severed Season:**
	* During the ancient Crown Wars, [[Corellon Larethian]] ripped a fifth, dark season (*Eclipse & Blight*) from the Feywild's magic forever after [[Lolth]]'s rebellion, banishing her into [[The Abyss]] and her followers into the [[Underdark]] as the first [[Elvenkind|Drow]].
