# Basic Info
- Government: [[Magocracy]]
- Leaders: Currently Unknown
- Alignment: Neutral

### General Info:
Porthca is widely considered one of the greatest cities, and regarded as the greatest centre of magical learning within the Eastern side of Nyropa.
The city's national philosophy is simple: '*Magic is a gift*'. Though interpreted differently throughout history, nearly every citizen grows up hearing these words.

Often noble families are renowned for what they have contributed towards knowledge of the Weave. Some fewer, more famous houses, are renowned for how they have wielded their knowledge of the Weave. And thus known for their magical accomplishments, rather than military or financial conquest.

This is typically seen as quite an 'exotic' culture in comparison to other cultures and cities across Eastern Nyropa.
### Founding - 823 BR
Formally founded in 823BR, by travellers fleeing [[The Tomb of Horrors]], and [[The Vale of Omens]] - [[Porthca - The Arcane Bridge|Porthca]], has always had a strong connection to the [[The Weave|Weave]]. Some of its founders went on to found [[Brugrove - City of the Iron Guard#Founding - 813 BR|Brugrove]] only 10yrs later (813BR).

Prior to this, Porthca had been known by many names and many conquerors, thus their culture has shifted over time, but never their arcane connection.
