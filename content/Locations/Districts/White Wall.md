A solemn feeling of judgement and destiny befalls all that enter the eerily quiet courtyard and chalk-white wall of the monastery looming over. Its surface salt streaked and pitted like worn bone.

Often, the silence surrounding the location is met by the bodies of the debted, hanged in silence. Their bodies swaying gently in the sea-breeze, others stiff as driftwood.

The characteristic toll of iron bells greets all that enter its bleak gates. Not bright nor joyous but a slow, deliberate, tolling. Each note like a nail in wood. The signal to all those that debts have been  collected.

Run by the vigil watchers of [[The Ledger]], often found in their ashen robes, lips sewn, tattoos seared away, faces emotionless, clutching books bound in rusted chain, using steel spikes for writing utensils, stand ever watching ready to write the next name. [[The Damned]].
