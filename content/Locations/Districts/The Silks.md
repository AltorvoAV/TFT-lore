Sitting almost at the heart the [[The Lost City - City of Thieves|Lost City]], [[The Silks]] is an illusion of beauty laden in perfume, silks, lanterns of various colours, and false faces.

It's a place of fortune tellers, pickpockets, gambling dens, performing arts, brothels and countless other [[The Silks|Velvet Lies]].

Run by the stunningly terrifying [[Madame Viatrix]] and occupied by the Con Artist's guild.
