The [[Charnel Steps]], aptly named after the bodies buried in its guild's creation, is home to [[The Black Gauntlets]], a brutal and terrifying guild of enforcers, lead by [[Nester Tarrenshade]].

[[Charnel Steps|The Steps]] welcomes all its visitors with black iron gate, the iconic blood-covered steps marking the only entrance and exit. The stench of blood, grime, and violence consume this district.
