Found on the outskirts of [[Brugrove - City of the Iron Guard|Brugrove]] and home to wayward travellers, the party stumbled across this during [[Act I]].

From the DM's notes:

> Alyssia and Dolomir make for the Stalker’s Tavern. Whilst the others sit and rest for a moment to gain their composure. Alyssia walk into the Tavern to see six hooded figures and a barmaid making up its inhabitancy. Dolomir goes to wipe his face of blood, and Alyssia scouts the room and orders drinks under Dolomir’s tab. Meanwhile, the others start to make their way. Looking at the sign to ‘The Lost Lair of Souls’, Drachen wonders if they should take the path… however a chill and fear unsettles his body giving him a feeling he has only felt once before. They continue.
> 
> With everyone now at the tavern and drinks ordered. They sit and eye up the cloak covered patrons. The barmaid edging ever closer to the table, asking what brings them this way. Talk about the Dr, which brings interest of the hooded folk. Drachen goes and sits with them. Tries to come to an agreement with Brian Blank and the idiot. They are told that Dr Iscara’s Wife & Daughter were killed by order of the King. Drachen is uncaring about this, showing no loyalty. A fight breaks out.
> 
> Drachen takes a couple of blows whilst dealing severe damage to those around him. All except Alyssia take on this fight. Skapa atop a table firing down. Drachen takes the dimwit which the part was to leave alive, bends him over a bar table and guts him like one of the Kobold he did only an hour ago. Amidst the fight, Alyssia is talking to the barmaid about her family and what her son is doing (husband is a miner, son is a coachman for the carriages carrying munitions to the war front). She also mentions that the people they were fighting with helped her get this bar and helped build the furniture in the building.
