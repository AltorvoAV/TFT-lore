- Government: [[Satrapy]] (Governed by [[Brugrove - City of the Iron Guard|Brugrove]])

Was a mining town, filled with people. Now, a wasteland of death, decay, and ruin.

Following [[Dr Zagak Iscara|Dr Iscara]]'s rampage, and the party's inability to stop his [[Well of Damnation]] ritual, it has sucked all the [[The Weave|Weave]] energy out of the surrounding 15mile radius, leaving it a [[Dead Zone]].
