## Basic Info
- Government: [[Vassal State]] of [[The Warrens of the Goblin King]]
- Leaders: [[Krevois Larkner]]
- Alignment: Unlawful

[[Arkford]] has the unlucky disposition of being placed between some of the worst and most bloodthirsty people who stop at nothing until they've pillaged and taken what they want.

[[The Warrens of the Goblin King]], [[The Desolation of Berossu]], and [[Grysev Castle]] all prey upon [[Arkford]] for its people, to add to their armies, their money, food, materials, and hope.

The gods have surely forsaken this place. It remains as nothing but a battleground for [[Gruumsh]]'s followers and for greed.
