#### Meaning:
[[Outer Planes]] is a collective term used to describe a subset of the known [[Realms|planes of existence]] in cosmology models.

The term refers to the placement of the planes being the outermost concentric sphere that makes up the multiverse in the Great Wheel cosmology model.

These 17 are:
- [[Acheron]] 
- [[Arcadia]] 
- [[Outlands]] (Concordant Opposition)
- [[Elysium]] 
- [[Gehenna]]
- [[Ysgard]] 
- [[Hades]] (The Gray Waste) 
- [[Beastlands]] (Happy Hunting Grounds)
- [[Limbo]] 
- [[Nine Hells]] (Baator) 
- [[Mechanus]] (Nirvana)
- [[Olympus]] (Arborea/Arvandor)
- [[Pandemonium]] 
- [[Mount Celestia]] (Seven Heavens) 
- [[Carceri]] (Tarterus) 
- [[Bytopia]] (Twin Paradises)
