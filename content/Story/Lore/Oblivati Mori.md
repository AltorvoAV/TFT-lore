Info read in a book within the Great [[Brugrove - City of the Iron Guard|Brugrovian]] Library, which expands on the knowledge shared by [[Aron Elliot]]:
### Summary:
“When a god is forgotten, they do not sleep — they cease. This law, whispered by clerics as **[[Oblivati Mori]]** (‘*to die forgotten*’), is the severest fate. Such a god cannot be resurrected by mortal means, nor can their name be recalled by mortal tongue. Only the divine remember.

When a god is slain yet remembered, their name lingers like a scar. Worshippers may fall to madness at the sudden silence, but should their faith endure, so too may their god.
Some scholars call this the [[Oblivati Mori|Law of Theothinatic Silence]] — the belief that divine names may be erased not by time, but by deliberate will.”
#### Artefacts:
Where a god's [[Artefacts]] remain, so too does the possibility of their resurrection.
#### Anchoring Divine Essence:
[[Oblivati Mori]] applies to deities whose divine essence is anchored to the planes. A being born upon the [[Material Plane]] that ascends into godhood remains materially anchored, and is therefore unaffected by [[Oblivati Mori]].

There are occurrences where deities have bound their essence to a plane not of their natural anchor in order to either survive physical death, or [[Oblivati Mori]].

A being can be protected from the finality of physical death or from Oblivion — but never both.

#### Further info given to the party by Drachen's [[Patron]]:

[[Oblivati Mori]] is the most severe fate that could behold a [[Entities/Gods/Gods|God]]. Once the name is scrubbed clean, almost nothing can bring them back. Though discovering the name, believing in it, and uttering it, can strengthen their connection to power, bringing them back.

It is possible to kill a god via removing any trace of their existence upon the mortal realms, but it takes *significant* power.

If you can remember a god's name, but none follow them, they are dormant and unaffected by [[Oblivati Mori]], for as long as their name remains in mortal minds.
