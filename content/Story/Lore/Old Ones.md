TLDR: Ancient, eldritch beings who offer patronage to certain mortals.

- The [[Old Ones]] are the name given to ancient, eldritch beings who offer patronage to mortals. They are beings of immense power, and accounts of every known [[Old Ones|Old One]] confirm that they once held divine status in some capacity. Though no longer recognised as conventional deities, their power remains, and they are more commonly regarded as cosmic beings.
	- The [[Old Ones]] possess knowledge accumulated over incomprehensible spans of time, far exceeding anything preserved within the greatest libraries of [[Nyropa]]. Those who receive their patronage may draw upon this knowledge, gaining access to powers and understanding far beyond what mortals could ordinarily achieve.
    - Given that every known [[Old Ones|Old One]] was held divine domains, it is commonly believed that they constitute some form of 'god-race'. It is theorised that they are an entirely separate race of beings, born specifically into godhood, before eventually becoming something different to conventional divinity. Others speculate that they are actually forgotten members of the [[Elder]] race, whose ancient history has been lost to time.
- The relationship between the Old Ones and the Elders remains a matter of speculation. There are few reliable historical records, and the beings themselves still remain poorly understood. Their origins, motivations and true nature are still largely unknown.
